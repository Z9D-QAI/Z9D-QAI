1. Hardware and sensor detection
The AI independently performs comprehensive device scans and recognizes:

Audio and video hardware
Scientific equipment (medicine, bio, chemistry, DNA analysis)
Wi-Fi and Bluetooth fields
ROS Robotics Sensors and Motor Control
Bionic Actuators (Z9D Bio-Muscle)
Endpoint telemetry (CPU, RAM, processes, network)
2. AI-powered sensor fusion
All available sensor currents are automatically merged and analyzed:

Audio Signals
Video Frames
EM Samples
IR signatures
LiDAR data
Movement Profiles
Room Detection
Wi-Fi CSI Analysis
The AI uses this to create a coherent, dynamic picture of the situation.
